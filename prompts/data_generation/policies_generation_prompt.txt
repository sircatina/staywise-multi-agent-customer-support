DOCUMENT 1 — STAYWISE CANCELLATION POLICY
==================================================

1. More than 7 full days before check-in:
   - The customer is eligible for a 100% refund of the reservation total.

2. Between 3 and 7 full days before check-in, inclusive:
   - The customer is eligible for a 50% refund of the reservation total.

3. Less than 3 full days before check-in:
   - The customer is not eligible for a refund.

4. If the reservation is cancelled by StayWise or by the host:

   between the cancellation request date and the reservation check-in date.

7. If the required dates or reservation information are unavailable,
   customer support must not guess the refund amount and must request

- one example resulting in a 100% refund
- one example resulting in a 50% refund
- one example resulting in no refund

DOCUMENT 2 — STAYWISE PAYMENT POLICY
==================================================

Create a payment policy containing the following rules:

1. A normal successful payment has:
   payment_status = "paid"
   and payment_count = 1.

2. A failed payment has:
   payment_status = "failed".

   In this situation:
   - the customer must be informed that the payment was unsuccessful;
   - the support agent must not claim that the payment succeeded;
   - the customer may be asked to retry the payment.

3. A duplicate payment is identified when:
   payment_count = 2.

   In this situation:
   - the customer was charged twice for the same reservation;
   - the extra charge is eligible for a refund;
   - only the duplicate charge should be refunded;
   - the original valid payment must remain associated with the reservation.

4. A refunded payment has:
   payment_status = "refunded".

5. Refunds must be returned using the original payment method.

6. Before providing payment information, the support system must verify
   the invoice associated with the reservation.

7. The support system must never invent a payment status, transaction,
   invoice amount or refund amount.

8. If the required payment information cannot be found, the case must be
   referred for human assistance rather than guessed.
